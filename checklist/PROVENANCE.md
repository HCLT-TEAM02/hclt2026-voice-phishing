# Provenance of chk_v3

A **48-question subset of chk_v2 (112 questions)**, selected by hand.

- The question wording is taken from chk_v2 verbatim (48/48 unchanged).
- **Question IDs were renumbered from Q01 within each sub-dimension.**
  The same ID therefore refers to different questions in chk_v2 and chk_v3.
  Example: D1-S2-Q01 in chk_v3 is D1-S2-Q02 in chk_v2.
- Never mix judgments across the two versions. They are separated by study_id.
- Composition: 4 dimensions / 17 sub-dimensions / 48 questions (D1 18, D2 12, D3 14, D4 4).
- prompts/ is identical to chk_v2, because those prompts produced the pool these 48 questions
  came from.
